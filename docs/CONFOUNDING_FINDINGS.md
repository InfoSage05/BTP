# Core findings — source-level confounding in consolidated CHF databases

Generated by `scripts/run_confounding_analysis.py` (seed 42, deterministic).
Raw tables in `results/confounding/`. This file is the human-readable record of
what those numbers mean and which of them are safe to put in the manuscript.

## 1. The dose-response spine of the paper

Three independent CHF data families, identical protocol, spanning three orders
of magnitude in sample size. `ICC` = share of log-CHF variance explained by
**study identity alone** (which laboratory produced the row).

| Family | n | studies | rows/study | ICC (study) | Features gain over study-ID alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ATF surface | 55 | 7 | 7.9 | **0.815** | **+0.042** |
| Pin-fin | 175 | 16 | 10.9 | 0.659 | +0.303 |
| NRC flow-boiling | 24,443 | 60 | 407 | 0.345 | +0.556 |

Monotonic in rows-per-study. On the ATF surface set, knowing *which lab ran the
test* explains 81.5% of CHF variance; adding roughness **and** contact angle on
top buys only 4.2 points more.

## 2. Simpson reversals — pooled analysis inverts the physics

`pooled_r` = correlation on the pooled data (what a naive study reports).
`within_r` = correlation after centring each feature and the target within study.

| Family | Feature | pooled r | within-study r | Reversal | Studies agreeing with within-study sign |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ATF surface | **Ra** | −0.293 | **+0.439** | **YES** | **5 / 5** |
| ATF surface | Contact angle | −0.774 | −0.332 | no (but 2.3× overstated) | 5 / 6 |
| Pin-fin | **Subcooling** | −0.092 | **+0.537** | **YES** | **6 / 6** |
| Pin-fin | Roughness factor | +0.156 | +0.466 | no (3× understated) | 13 / 13 |
| Pin-fin | Porosity | +0.456 | +0.671 | no | 7 / 7 |
| NRC flow | all 7 features | — | — | **none** | 13–56 / 19–58 |

The ATF roughness reversal matters physically: pooled data says *rougher
surfaces have lower CHF*, contradicting the capillary-wicking mechanism that
every one of those source papers invokes. Within-study, all five testable
studies show the physically expected **positive** roughness effect.

**No reversals occur in the large NRC set.** The pathology is specific to
sparse, few-rows-per-study compilations.

## 3. The generalisation collapse

Leave-one-study-out (RandomForest, log CHF). R² < 0 means *worse than
predicting the global mean*.

**ATF surface — every testable held-out laboratory fails:**

| Held-out study | n_test | R² |
|---|---|---|
| Ahn2010 | 10 | −10.65 |
| He2022 | 27 | −6.37 |
| Kam2015 | 3 | −3.72 |
| Yeom2020 | 9 | −3.17 |
| Ali2020 | 4 | −1.53 |
| Ali2018 / Kim2019 | 1 each | not scoreable |

Under random 5-fold CV the same data gives R² ≈ 0.63–0.70 across all five
model families. **That gap — 0.70 down to negative — is the headline.**

**Pin-fin — generalises to most labs:** 11 of 16 held-out studies score
R² 0.81–0.97; three are negative (Guglielmini −1.53, Nguyen −0.61, Kim −0.44).

**NRC flow — optimism gap is small:** random 0.99 vs grouped 0.90–0.96
(gap 0.035–0.090 depending on model).

## 4. Why the three families differ — the constructive result

This is what turns a critique into a contribution. The ATF surface models carry
only **two** covariates (Ra, contact angle). Those cannot distinguish a
horizontally-placed tube at 84 kPa in Albuquerque from a flat plate at
101 kPa in Seoul — so the model has no way to explain inter-laboratory offsets
and is forced to memorise them. Pin-fin carries geometry, porosity, coverage
**and fluid**; NRC carries full thermal-hydraulics. Both generalise.

**Prescription:** when leave-one-study-out fails, the remedy is not more rows
from the same laboratories — it is richer per-row covariates (heater geometry,
system pressure, substrate construction, thermal effusivity) that let a model
account for inter-laboratory variation instead of absorbing it into the
surface-feature coefficients.

## 5. Caveats to state explicitly in the manuscript

- **ATF set is imbalanced**: He2022 supplies 27 of 55 modellable rows (49%);
  two studies contribute a single row and cannot be held out at all.
- **The ATF roughness reversal is partly driven by high-roughness sandblasted
  samples.** Excluding Ra > 10 µm, the within-study correlation falls to +0.068
  (from +0.439). The reversal direction is robust to dropping any single study,
  but the effect magnitude is not robust to excluding the sandblasted arm. Report both.
- **ANN under grouped CV is unstable** on 55 rows: the He2022 fold returns
  R² = −418. Report median-across-folds or per-fold values, never a single mean,
  and say why.
- **Zhang2023 contributes no roughness values** (not published) so it drops out
  of any Ra analysis; Kim2019 and Son2019 per-specimen CHF are figure-only.
- Correlations are Pearson on log CHF; the small-n per-study correlations
  (n = 3–4) are indicative only and are reported with their n attached.
