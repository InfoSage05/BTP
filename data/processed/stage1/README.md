# Stage 1 results — architecture bake-off (MLP vs. Transformer)

First-pass comparison, CPU-only, fast config (6 pretrain epochs, no
hyperparameter search, monotonicity penalty applied only during fine-tuning
not pretraining). Not final numbers — a directional read to decide where to
invest further tuning.

## Splits
Both drawn from the cleaned real core dataset (24,579-row NRC database,
`data/raw/pretraining/nrc_groeneveld_24579pt_chf_database.csv`):

- **Interpolation**: random 80/10/10 train/val/test.
- **Extrapolation**: train/val only on P <= 8 MPa (9,207 / 1,624 rows),
  test entirely on P > 8 MPa (13,748 rows, never seen during training).

The Stage 0 synthetic pretraining corpus (300k rows) is NOT pressure-cut —
it covers the LUT's full range on purpose, so the model sees the correct
physical trend across all pressures before fine-tuning narrows to real,
low-pressure-only data for the extrapolation run.

## Results

| Architecture | Split | n (test) | R2 | rRMSE | MAPE % | within +-10% | RMSE kW/m2 | wall time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MLP | interp | 2,459 | 0.9515 | 0.308 | 17.9% | 47.3% | 358 | 65s |
| Transformer | interp | 2,459 | 0.9568 | 0.268 | 16.5% | 47.7% | 338 | 32.5 min |
| MLP | extrap | 13,748 | 0.8381 | 0.778 | 44.6% | 25.8% | 459 | 25s |
| Transformer | extrap | 13,748 | 0.8437 | 0.797 | **49.5%** | **20.2%** | 451 | 16.8 min |

## Reading, as of this run
On extrapolation (the split that matters), R2 is a near-tie (+0.006 for the
Transformer), but the Transformer is *worse* on MAPE and the within-+-10%
rate -- on typical predictions, not just the aggregate R2, the MLP is doing
better here, at roughly 1/40th the training cost. No evidence yet that the
Transformer's extra capacity is paying off on this dataset/config.

This is not a final verdict on the Transformer -- it's one fast, untuned
run. Known reasons the Transformer might still pull ahead with more
investment: a proper hyperparameter search (d_model, depth, heads), more
pretraining epochs on the full 300k-row synthetic corpus, GPU-scale
training allowing much larger pretraining corpora and architecture search
per the plan discussed in this project. Kept on file specifically to
revisit once GPU access is available.

## Also notable
Both architectures land below the literature's Yang et al. (2025)
benchmark (R2 0.91-0.96 at a comparable pressure cutoff) -- expected, since
this was a fast first-pass config, not a tuned final model. That gap is the
target for the next round, not a sign of a broken pipeline.
